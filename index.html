Sacred Rituals

The divine ceremonies of our wedding

1

Samaa Varthanamu

The sacred ritual of purification, preparing the couple for the divine union ahead.

2

Kaasi Yaatra

The groom pretends to leave for Kashi (Varanasi) for ascetic life, but is persuaded by the bride's father to embrace married life.

3

Vivaaha Deeksha Sweekaranam

The formal acceptance of the marriage vow, marking the couple's commitment to the sacred bond.

4

Yognopaveetha Dhaaranam

The sacred thread ceremony, symbolizing spiritual readiness and divine blessings.

5

Gruhasthaasrama Pramaanam Sweekaranam

Accepting the responsibilities and joys of Gruhasthaasramam — the householder's life.

6

Kanya Varanam

The groom formally seeks the bride's hand, a beautiful moment of consent and acceptance.

7

Vara Pooja

The bride's family worships the groom, honoring him as Lord Vishnu himself.

8

Kaallu Kaduguta

The sacred washing of the groom's feet by the bride's parents as a mark of respect.

9

Kanyadhaanam

The most sacred moment — the bride's parents give their daughter's hand, the greatest of all gifts.

10

MadhuParkamu

Offering a sacred mixture of honey and curd to the groom, symbolizing sweetness in married life.

11

MaangalyaDhaaranam

The most auspicious moment — tying the sacred Mangalsutra, the eternal bond of marriage.

12

Akshathaaropanamu

Showering the couple with sacred rice and turmeric, blessing them with prosperity.

13

Bramhamudi

Tying the sacred knot — the garments of the bride and groom are tied together, symbolizing their inseparable union.

14

Homamu

The sacred fire ritual — offerings are made to Agni Dev, the divine witness of the marriage.

15

PaaniGrahanamu

The groom holds the bride's hand, promising to walk through life together.

16

Saptapadhi

The seven sacred steps around the holy fire — each step a vow, each vow a lifetime promise.

17

Nakshatra Dharsanam

Viewing the Arundhati star together — symbolizing eternal devotion and fidelity, like the star that never fades.
